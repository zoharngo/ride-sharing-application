# RideIL

A mobile taxi-dispatch platform connecting passengers with licensed taxi drivers in Israel.

---

## About

RideIL is a legally compliant ride-hailing service built on Israel's taxi-dispatch model — the only approved framework for paid ride-hailing as of January 2026. The platform is available on iOS and Android and supports Hebrew, Arabic, and English.

The project is designed with full regulatory compliance in mind, including the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law (Amendment 13) and Ministry of Transportation driver and vehicle licensing requirements.

## Features

**Planned capabilities:**

- **Taxi-dispatch compliance** — Operates under the only legally approved model for paid ride-hailing in Israel
- **Privacy by design** — Compliant with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law (Amendment 13)
- **Licensed drivers and vehicles** — Verified against Ministry of Transportation licensing requirements
- **Cross-platform** — Native iOS and Android applications
- **Multilingual** — Full support for Hebrew, Arabic, and English
- **Safe and transparent rides** — Real-time tracking, fare transparency, and driver ratings
- **Future-ready architecture** — Designed to activate private-driver features when legislation permits
