How It Works

Get a ride in three simple steps

Open the app, set your destination, and let RideIL match you with a nearby licensed driver.

1

Set Your Pickup

Enter your destination or drop a pin. RideIL auto-detects your location for faster booking.

2

Get Matched

3

Ride & Pay

Features

Built for Israel, built to last

Every feature is designed around safety, transparency, and full compliance with Israeli law.

Fully Legal

Operates under Israel's taxi-dispatch regulations — the only legally approved model for paid ride-hailing as of 2026.

Privacy Protected

Built from the ground up to comply with Israel's Protection of Privacy Law (Amendment 13). Your data stays yours.

Verified Drivers

Every driver and vehicle is verified against Ministry of Transportation licensing requirements before joining the platform.

Cross-Platform

Available on both iOS and Android with a consistent, native-quality experience across every device.

Trilingual

Full interface support in Hebrew, Arabic, and English — including right-to-left layouts — so every community is served.

Future-Ready

Designed to support private-driver features the moment Israeli legislation evolves. We're ready for what's next.
