## 5. Location Data

### 5.1 How We Collect Location Data

We collect precise GPS location data from your device when you use the RideIL app. This includes:

- **Passengers:** Location collected when requesting a ride and during active trips
- **Drivers:** Location collected continuously while online to enable matching

### 5.2 Location Data Retention

| Stage | Retention Period | Data Precision |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| Active Trip | Real-time | Precise GPS coordinates |
| Post-Trip (Dispute Period) | 30 days | Precise GPS coordinates |
| After Dispute Period | 2 years | Aggregated/anonymized only |

### 5.3 Location Data Access

Access to your location data is strictly controlled:
- You can view your own trip history
- Drivers can view their own trip history
- Support staff access trip data only for complaint investigation
- Compliance reports use anonymized/aggregated data

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Under Amendment 13 to the Protection of Privacy Law, you have the following rights:

### 9.1 Right of Access
You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
- **How to exercise:** In-app (Profile > Privacy > Download My Data) or email privacy@rideil.co.il
- **Response time:** 30 days

### 9.2 Right to Rectification
You can correct inaccurate personal data.
- **How to exercise:** Edit in-app or contact support
- **Response time:** 14 days

### 9.3 Right to Erasure
You can request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
- **How to exercise:** In-app (Profile > Privacy > Delete Account) or email privacy@rideil.co.il
- **Response time:** 30 days
- **Limitations:** Trip records may be retained for legal compliance

### 9.4 Right to Restriction
You can request temporary restriction of processing.
- **How to exercise:** Email privacy@rideil.co.il
- **Response time:** 72 hours

### 9.5 Right to Data Portability
You can receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON or CSV).
- **How to exercise:** In-app or email privacy@rideil.co.il
- **Response time:** 30 days

### 9.6 Right to Object
You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **How to exercise:** Email privacy@rideil.co.il
- **Note:** Essential service processing cannot be objected to
